Merge Rules

Name Priority:
1. Aadhaar
2. Passport
3. PAN

DOB Priority:
1. Aadhaar
2. Birth Certificate
3. Passport

Address Priority:
1. Aadhaar Back
2. Utility Bill
3. Bank Passbook

Numbers:
- PAN Number: Only from PAN Card
- Passport Number: Only from Passport
- Driving Licence Number: Only from Driving Licence
- Aadhaar Number: Only from Aadhaar Card

Contact:
- Phone: Prefer Aadhaar, otherwise Utility Bill
- Email: Use only if explicitly visible on the document.
